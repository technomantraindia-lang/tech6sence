What are AIOps & MLOps?
AIOps
AIOps applies Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning to IT operations by analyzing logs, metrics, traces, events, and infrastructure telemetry to automate monitoring, incident detection, root cause analysis, anomaly detection, predictive maintenance, and operational workflows.
MLOps
MLOps is the discipline of managing the complete machine learning lifecycle—from data preparation and model development to deployment, monitoring, governance, retraining, and retirement—using automation, DevOps practices, and enterprise governance.
Together, AIOps and MLOps create intelligent, resilient, and continuously optimized operational environments for both IT infrastructure and AI systems.

What is Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)?
Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) is an advanced AI architecture that combines information retrieval with Large Language Models.
A typical RAG workflow includes:
User submits a question.
The system retrieves the most relevant enterprise information.
Retrieved content is securely provided to the language model.
The AI generates a context-aware response grounded in retrieved knowledge.
Responses may include references, supporting context, or traceability where appropriate.
Unlike traditional Generative AI, RAG enables AI systems to:
Access enterprise knowledge
Retrieve live information
Generate grounded responses
Improve factual accuracy
Support explainability
Minimize hallucinations
Deliver domain-specific intelligence

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)
What is Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)?
Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) is an AI architecture that combines information retrieval with Large Language Models to generate responses based on relevant enterprise knowledge retrieved at query time, improving accuracy, context, and explainability.
Why is RAG better than using a standalone Large Language Model?
Standalone language models rely primarily on their training data and may produce outdated or unsupported responses. RAG augments the model with current, organization-specific information, helping improve factual grounding, reduce hallucinations, and provide context-aware answers.
Can you build secure RAG solutions using our private enterprise data?
Yes. We develop secure RAG platforms that integrate with enterprise document repositories, business applications, databases, cloud storage, and APIs while respecting organizational access controls and security policies.
Do you support multimodal and Graph RAG?
Absolutely. We develop Multimodal RAG solutions that retrieve information from text, images, audio, video, and technical documents, as well as Graph RAG platforms that combine Knowledge Graphs with language models for deeper reasoning and relationship analysis.
Do you provide long-term support after deployment?
Yes. We provide managed RAG services including knowledge synchronization, embedding updates, retrieval optimization, prompt engineering, model monitoring, governance reviews, security updates, and continuous platform enhancement.
